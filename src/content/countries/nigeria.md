## Why Nigeria?

The Nigeria passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **89** destinations, ranking it **#190** globally.

> Dual only for Nigerian citizens by birth.

## Citizenship by descent

Nigeria applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **15 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Nigeria generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

## Why Chad?

The Chad passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **97** destinations, ranking it **#173** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Chad applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

## Dual citizenship

Chad generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Why Chad?

The Chad passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **97** destinations, ranking it **#173** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Chad applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **15 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Chad generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

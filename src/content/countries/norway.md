## Why Norway?

The Norway passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **185** destinations, ranking it **#3** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Norway applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **7 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Norway generally permits dual citizenship.

## Why France?

The France passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **184** destinations, ranking it **#23** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

France applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence (reduced to **4 years** for spouses).

France typically requires **B1** language proficiency for naturalisation.

## Dual citizenship

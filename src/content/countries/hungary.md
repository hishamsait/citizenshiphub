## Why Hungary?

The Hungary passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **185** destinations, ranking it **#3** globally.

> Descent/simplified naturalisation for ethnic Hungarians.

## Citizenship by descent

Hungary applies jus sanguinis with no generational limit citizenship can be passed down indefinitely through ancestry.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **8 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Hungary generally permits dual citizenship.

## Investment routes

Hungary offers a **golden visa** (residency by investment) (from ≈ **€250,000** in qualifying investment) a residence permit leading to naturalisation.

## Other routes

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

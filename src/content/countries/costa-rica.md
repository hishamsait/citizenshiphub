## Why Costa Rica?

The Costa Rica passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **170** destinations, ranking it **#55** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Costa Rica applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **7 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Costa Rica generally permits dual citizenship.

## Other routes

Citizenship is granted by **birth in the territory** (jus soli).

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

- Corporate income tax rate: **30%**
- Standard VAT/GST rate: **13%**
- Taxation basis: **territorial** (residents are generally taxed only on domestic-source income)

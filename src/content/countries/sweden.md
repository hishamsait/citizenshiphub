## Why Sweden?

The Sweden passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **185** destinations, ranking it **#3** globally.

> No citizenship test; fee SEK 1,500 (≈ €130).

## Citizenship by descent

Sweden applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Sweden generally permits dual citizenship.

## Why Cape Verde?

The Cape Verde passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **108** destinations, ranking it **#131** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Cape Verde applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Cape Verde generally permits dual citizenship.

## Other routes

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

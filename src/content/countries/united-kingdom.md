## Why United Kingdom?

The United Kingdom passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **180** destinations, ranking it **#41** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

United Kingdom applies jus sanguinis citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

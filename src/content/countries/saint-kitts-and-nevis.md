## Why Saint Kitts and Nevis?

The Saint Kitts and Nevis passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **170** destinations, ranking it **#55** globally.

> Also citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship by descent

Saint Kitts and Nevis applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Saint Kitts and Nevis generally permits dual citizenship.

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

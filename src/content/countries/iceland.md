## Why Iceland?

The Iceland passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **184** destinations, ranking it **#23** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Iceland applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **7 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Iceland generally permits dual citizenship.

## Other routes

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

## Fees & timeline

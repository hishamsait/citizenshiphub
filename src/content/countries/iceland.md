## Why Iceland?

The Iceland passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **184** destinations, ranking it **#23** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Iceland applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **7 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Iceland generally permits dual citizenship.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

## Why North Korea?

The North Korea passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **91** destinations, ranking it **#186** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

North Korea applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation in North Korea is restricted or not standardised — there is no straightforward residency-based route for most people.

## Dual citizenship

North Korea generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Fees & timeline

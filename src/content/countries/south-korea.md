## Citizenship by descent

South Korea applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

South Korea generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

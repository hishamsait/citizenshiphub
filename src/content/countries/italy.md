## Why Italy?

The Italy passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **184** destinations, ranking it **#23** globally.

> Descent via ancestry (jure sanguinis, no generational limit); 10 yrs residence.

## Citizenship by descent

Italy applies jus sanguinis with no generational limit — citizenship can be passed down indefinitely through ancestry.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **10 years** of residence (reduced to **2 years** for spouses).

Italy typically requires **B1** language proficiency for naturalisation.

## Dual citizenship

Italy generally permits dual citizenship.

## Investment routes

Italy offers a **golden visa** (residency by investment) (from ≈ **€250,000** in qualifying investment) — a residence permit leading to naturalisation.

## Other routes

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

## Fees & timeline

The application fee is around **€250** (approximate).

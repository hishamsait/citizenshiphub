## Why Andorra?

The Andorra passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **176** destinations, ranking it **#47** globally.

> Long residency (20 yrs); dual citizenship generally not allowed.

## Citizenship by descent

Andorra applies jus sanguinis citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **20 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Andorra generally does not permit dual citizenship naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Investment routes

Andorra offers a **golden visa** (residency by investment) (from ≈ **€600,000** in qualifying investment) a residence permit leading to naturalisation.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee costs vary, so check with official sources.

- Top personal income tax rate: **10%**
- Corporate income tax rate: **10%**
- Standard VAT/GST rate: **4.5%**
- Taxation basis: **worldwide** (residents are generally taxed on worldwide income)

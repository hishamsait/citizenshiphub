## Why Netherlands?

The Netherlands passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **185** destinations, ranking it **#3** globally.

> Dual citizenship restricted (exceptions apply); fee ≈ €1,030.

## Citizenship by descent

Netherlands applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Netherlands generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

The application fee is around **€1030** (approximate).

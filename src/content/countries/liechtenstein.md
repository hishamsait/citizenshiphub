## Why Liechtenstein?

The Liechtenstein passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **182** destinations, ranking it **#38** globally.

> Very long residency; dual citizenship restricted.

## Citizenship by descent

Liechtenstein applies jus sanguinis citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **30 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Liechtenstein generally does not permit dual citizenship naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Fees & timeline

## Why Taiwan?

The Taiwan passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **157** destinations, ranking it **#81** globally.

> Naturalised citizens generally must renounce prior nationality.

## Citizenship by descent

Taiwan applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Taiwan generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Other routes

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

## Tax considerations

- Top personal income tax rate: **40%**
- Corporate income tax rate: **20%**
- Standard VAT/GST rate: **5%**
- Taxation basis: **worldwide** (residents are generally taxed on worldwide income)

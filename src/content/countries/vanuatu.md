## Why Vanuatu?

The Vanuatu passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **123** destinations, ranking it **#105** globally.

> Also citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship by descent

Vanuatu applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **10 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Vanuatu generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

## Why Spain?

Spanish nationality gives you full EU citizenship and one of the world's strongest passports (see the mobility panel). Two features stand out: a **2-year fast-track** naturalisation for citizens of Latin America and a handful of other countries, and the **Democratic Memory Law**, which opens a descent route for descendants of Spanish exiles.

## Citizenship by descent

Spain applies **jus sanguinis**:

- A child born to a Spanish **father or mother** is Spanish by origin, regardless of birthplace.
- Children born abroad to a Spanish parent can often acquire nationality by **option** within set time limits.
- **Democratic Memory Law (2022):** descendants of Spaniards who went into exile during the Civil War (1936-1939) and the Franco dictatorship can apply for Spanish nationality - effectively a grandparent-level descent route.

There is no general "any ancestor" programme; the exile route is the main exception.

## Naturalisation

The standard residence requirement is **10 years** of legal, continuous residence, plus proof of good civic conduct and integration.

It is reduced to **2 years** for citizens of Ibero-American countries, Andorra, the Philippines, Equatorial Guinea, Portugal, and Sephardic Jews. It drops to **1 year** for people born in Spain, married to a Spanish national, or born abroad to a Spanish parent or grandparent.

## Dual citizenship

Spain generally **requires you to renounce** your previous nationality when naturalising. The key exception: nationals of **Ibero-American countries, Andorra, the Philippines, Equatorial Guinea, and Portugal** may keep their original citizenship.

## Fees & timeline

The application fee is around **€100** (plus document and translation costs). Processing typically takes **one to three years** depending on the route and caseload.

## Why Portugal?

The Portugal passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **185** destinations, ranking it **#3** globally.

> Descent to grandparents (and Sephardic heritage); fee ≈ €250.

## Citizenship by descent

Portugal applies jus sanguinis citizenship can be acquired by descent from a parent or grandparent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence (reduced to **3 years** for spouses).

Portugal typically requires **A2** language proficiency for naturalisation.

## Dual citizenship

Portugal generally permits dual citizenship.

## Investment routes

Portugal offers a **golden visa** (residency by investment) (from ≈ **€500,000** in qualifying investment) a residence permit leading to naturalisation.

## Other routes

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

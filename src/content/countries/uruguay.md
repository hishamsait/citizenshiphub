## Why Uruguay?

The Uruguay passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **172** destinations, ranking it **#51** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Uruguay applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **3 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Uruguay generally permits dual citizenship.

## Other routes

Citizenship is granted by **birth in the territory** (jus soli).

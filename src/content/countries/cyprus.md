## Why Cyprus?

The Cyprus passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **183** destinations, ranking it **#35** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Cyprus applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **7 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Cyprus generally permits dual citizenship.

## Investment routes

Cyprus offers a **golden visa** (residency by investment) (from ≈ **€300,000** in qualifying investment) — a residence permit leading to naturalisation.

## Other routes

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

## Tax considerations

- Top personal income tax rate: **35%**
- Corporate income tax rate: **12.5%**
- Standard VAT/GST rate: **19%**
- Taxation basis: **worldwide** (residents are generally taxed on worldwide income)
- A **non-domiciled (remittance-basis)** tax regime is available to certain residents

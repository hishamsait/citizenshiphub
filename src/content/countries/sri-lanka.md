## Why Sri Lanka?

## Citizenship by descent

Sri Lanka applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Sri Lanka generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

## Fees & timeline

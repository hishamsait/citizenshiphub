The Namibia passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **111** destinations, ranking it **#122** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Namibia applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **10 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Namibia generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

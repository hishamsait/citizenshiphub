## Why Laos?

## Citizenship by descent

Laos applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

## Dual citizenship

Laos generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

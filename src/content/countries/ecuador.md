## Why Ecuador?

The Ecuador passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **122** destinations, ranking it **#106** globally.

## Citizenship by descent

Ecuador applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **3 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Ecuador generally permits dual citizenship.

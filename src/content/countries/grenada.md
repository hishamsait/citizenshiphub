## Why Grenada?

The Grenada passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **165** destinations, ranking it **#64** globally.

> Also citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship by descent

Grenada applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Grenada generally permits dual citizenship.

## Investment routes

Grenada offers **citizenship by investment** (from ≈ **€138,000** in qualifying investment) — a direct route to citizenship through investment.

## Other routes

Citizenship is granted by **birth in the territory** (jus soli).

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.

## Fees & timeline

There is no standard published application fee — costs vary, so check with official sources.

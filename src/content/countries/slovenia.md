## Why Slovenia?

The Slovenia passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **185** destinations, ranking it **#3** globally.

> Dual citizenship restricted.

## Citizenship by descent

Slovenia applies jus sanguinis — citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent, and in some cases from grandparents or more distant ancestors.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **10 years** of residence, usually alongside language and integration requirements.

## Dual citizenship

Slovenia generally does not permit dual citizenship — naturalising usually requires renouncing your existing nationality.

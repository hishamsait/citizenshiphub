## Why Antigua and Barbuda?

The Antigua and Barbuda passport offers visa-free, visa-on-arrival, ETA, or e-visa access to **168** destinations, ranking it **#59** globally.

> Also citizenship by investment.

## Citizenship by descent

Antigua and Barbuda applies jus sanguinis citizenship can be acquired by descent from a citizen parent.

## Naturalisation

Naturalisation typically requires **5 years** of residence.

## Dual citizenship

Antigua and Barbuda generally permits dual citizenship.

## Investment routes

Antigua and Barbuda offers **citizenship by investment** (from ≈ **€92,000** in qualifying investment) a direct route to citizenship through investment.

## Other routes

Citizenship is granted by **birth in the territory** (jus soli).

A **digital-nomad / remote-work visa** is available.
